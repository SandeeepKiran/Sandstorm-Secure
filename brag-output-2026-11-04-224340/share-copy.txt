Sandstorm Secure locks down Android phones through Google's own Android Management API: flip a switch and the policy JSON rewrites itself, scan one QR code to enroll, and see which phones comply. The dashboard runs only on 127.0.0.1, and wiping a phone means typing its device ID first. Try the sample-data demo: https://sandeeepkiran.github.io/Sandstorm-Secure/ (open source, AGPL-3.0)
