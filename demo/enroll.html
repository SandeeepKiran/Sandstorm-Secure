Enroll a device

Make a one-hour QR code. Scan it on the Pixel_6 emulator to put that device under a policy.

Use the Pixel_6 emulator only. Do not enroll a personal phone. A fully managed enrollment gives the dashboard control of the whole device.

  1. 1 Choose modeFully managed or work profile
  2. 2 Generate QRGoogle makes a one-hour token
  3. 3 Scan on the emulatorFrom the setup wizard

How to scan on the emulator

  1. For fully managed: factory-reset the emulator. On the Welcome screen, tap the screen 6 times to open the QR scanner.
  2. For work profile: open the emulator's camera or setup prompt on a device that is already set up.
  3. Point the emulator's virtual camera at the QR code. Follow the prompts.
  4. Open Devices and select Refresh to see the new device.
