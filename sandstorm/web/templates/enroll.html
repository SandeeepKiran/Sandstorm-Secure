How to scan on the emulator

  1. For fully managed: factory-reset the emulator. On the Welcome screen, tap the screen 6 times to open the QR scanner.
  2. For work profile: open the emulator's camera or setup prompt on a device that is already set up.
  3. Point the emulator's virtual camera at the QR code. Follow the prompts.
  4. Open Devices and select Refresh to see the new device.
